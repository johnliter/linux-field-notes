OUR PRINCIPLES

No magic commands.
Just reproducible fixes.

01 /

Symptoms first

We describe what broke and what the machine actually reported before suggesting a change.

02 /

Smallest safe change

We prefer targeted recovery steps over system-wide resets, reinstallations, or deleting configuration files.

03 /

Evidence over certainty

We clearly distinguish a confirmed recovery from a suspected root cause, and note what remains untested.
